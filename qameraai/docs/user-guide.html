Qamera AI for PrestaShop

Generate product packshots and photo sessions from the product page, and publish the ones you approve straight into the product gallery.

Module qameraai 1.0.0 · PrestaShop 8.x and 9.x · Academic Free License 3.0

1. What the module does

The module adds a Qamera AI tab to the product page in your back office. From that one tab you turn a photo you already have into a clean packshot, generate a photo session from that packshot, review the results, and publish the ones you like into the product gallery. You never leave the back office and nothing is uploaded to your shop until you accept it.

Generation runs on your Qamera AI account and consumes its credits. The module stores no generated content of its own — it reads the state of every job from Qamera AI, so what you see in the tab is always what the service actually holds.

3. Installing

  1. In the back office go to Modules → Module Manager → Upload a module and pick qameraai.zip.
  2. Open Configure on the installed module.
  3. Paste your API key and save. The page then shows your account name, plan and credit balance — that confirms the key works.
  4. Choose an AI model and save. This one is required: generation stays blocked until a model is selected.
  5. Optionally pick a default preset, which pre-selects a style for new sessions.

Leave API address at its default. It exists for development environments only.

4. Generating: the six steps

Step 1 — add a photo to the product. Use PrestaShop's own Images tab. The module works from the photos already in the product gallery.

Step 2 — choose the photo's role. Open the Qamera AI tab. Under Product photos, pick an image and add it either as a source photo, which is what you generate a packshot from, or directly as a finished packshot if the shot is already clean.

Step 3 — generate the packshot. Press Generate packshot on the source photo. The photo is sent to Qamera AI, analysed, and a packshot is generated. This takes up to a few minutes; the tab keeps checking and updates itself when the result arrives. You can leave the page open.

Step 4 — accept or reject the packshot. An accepted packshot becomes the base for photo sessions. Rejecting removes it from your Qamera AI catalogue.

Step 5 — generate a photo session. On an accepted packshot press Generate session. The settings panel on the left controls the whole session: style preset, model, scenery, aspect ratio, how many images to produce, and a free-text note if you want to steer the result.

One rule worth knowing: a session is always generated from a packshot, never straight from a source photo. That is deliberate — the packshot is what makes the results consistent, so the module will refuse a session started from a raw photo.

Step 6 — publish what you like. Press Accept under a session image and it is added to the product gallery and appears in your shop. Accepting the same image twice never creates a duplicate.

5. Credits

Every generated image costs credits on your Qamera AI account. The current balance is shown on the module's configuration page. When the balance runs out, generation stops with a clear message and nothing is charged twice — a repeated request for the same generation is recognised and not billed again.

6. When something goes wrong

What you seeWhat it means
No API key…The key is missing from the module configuration.
Invalid or unauthorised API key…The key is wrong, revoked or expired. Generate a new one in your Qamera AI account.
No credits left…Top up the account, then retry.
Too many requests…A rate limit. Wait a moment and try again.
Qamera AI server error…A problem on the service side. Failed generations are retried automatically; refresh the tab to see the current state.
Cannot reach Qamera AI…Your shop cannot make outgoing HTTPS requests. Check the server's network and that the cURL extension is enabled.
Generation did not succeed…The image could not be generated. Try a different source photo — sharp, well-lit, product filling the frame.

The tab always rebuilds its state from Qamera AI, so reloading the product page is safe at any point and never loses a result.

7. What the module does not do

Deliberate boundaries of this version: no generation across many products at once, no per-combination images, no multistore, no editing or regenerating a finished result, and no public callback endpoint — results are collected by the tab itself. Interface languages are Polish and English.

8. Data and privacy

The module sends to Qamera AI only what generation needs: the product photo you select, the product name, and an identifier that ties the results back to that product. Your API key is stored in the shop configuration and is never exposed to the browser — every call to Qamera AI is made by the server. Generated images are downloaded into your own gallery when you accept them. Uninstalling removes the module's local mapping tables; material already generated stays in your Qamera AI account.
